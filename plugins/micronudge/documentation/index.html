User guide

Ullhexa MicroNudge Use Ullhexa MicroNudge on your bass or other main track. Route the kick or reference track to the plugin's sidechain input to compare their waveforms. Sidechain routing depends on your DAW; it supplies a visual reference and is not mixed into the output by the scope's Sum display. Offset and phase OFFSET moves the main signal by up to 5 ms in either direction. PHASE rotates its phase from −180° to +180°. Each section has L/R controls, a Link option and a copy button that makes R match L. Click OFFSET or PHASE to bypass that section. Polarity inverts the signal, Stereo Flip swaps the channels, and Mono Sum combines them. Ullhexa MicroNudge reports its processing latency to the host. At 48 kHz it reports 1,775 samples. Keep the DAW's plugin delay compensation enabled for alignment and renders; low-latency monitoring and bypass behavior depend on the host. Offset is an intentional timing change in addition to compensated processing latency. Scope Scope opens the clock-synced display. Main, SC and Sum independently show the main output, sidechain and their visual sum. Mono or Stereo selects the view; Stereo offers Stacked and Overlay. Freeze holds the captured audio; offset and phase adjustments can still update the frozen preview. Use the bottom handle for time division and Dot or Trip for dotted or triplet timing. Use the right handle or scroll over the display for amplitude zoom. These display controls do not change output gain. VCA envelopes VCA enables project-synced attenuation. The top of the editor is unity gain; lower nodes reduce volume. Drag nodes, double-click to add or remove interior nodes, and drag a line to curve its segment. Up to 16 nodes are available. The two edge nodes remain at the cycle boundaries and share a level. Nodes can share a horizontal position for vertical steps. The green snap control and grid count apply to both bands. The curve button opens Factory, User and Favorites libraries. Selecting a shape applies it and closes the library. Save stores an edited shape; hover over its tile to reveal the favorite star. The smoothing slider softens the applied envelope without changing the authored shape. Reset resets the selected band's curve. At the left end of the HPF control, the band split is bypassed and the white envelope affects the full signal. Drag the actual filter handle to enable the split and change its cutoff. Click the purple or white region to edit the low or high band. Each band has independent time division and Dot/Trip settings. The small timing-link square copies the selected timing to the other band and keeps them linked while lit; it does not copy the curve. Presets and interface Click the preset name at the top to open your preset library. Save presets, mark favorites and organize references to the same preset in multiple groups. Removing from a group does not delete the preset; deleting permanently removes it from the library and its groups. Unsaved curve shapes used in a saved preset are also saved to the user curve library. The Ull Hexa logo opens About, UI controls and Scaling. Select a scale or drag the bottom-right corner; use Set current scale to default to remember it. Click outside a menu to close it. The curve library closes when a curve is chosen. Presets and curves are stored locally in ~/Library/Application Support/Ull Hexa/MicroNudge. Interface preferences are in ~/Library/Application Support/Ull Hexa/MicroNudge.settings. The plugin does not require an account or internet connection. The website button opens your browser when clicked. https://ullhexa.com
