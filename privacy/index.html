Privacy & cookies

Updated 20 November 2026

Ull Hexa handles website enquiries and the contribution records it receives. For questions or requests, use the contact form or the email address below.

Enquiries

The contact form sends your name, email, subject and message through Web3Forms to Gmail. Web3Forms also processes your IP address and submission time for delivery and spam filtering. Contact is voluntary. I need your email to reply. I use these details to answer enquiries and support requests (legitimate interests), or to take steps towards a contract at your request.

I keep ordinary messages for up to 12 months after the last reply. For active projects, the 12 months start when the project and related support end. Accounting or dispute records are kept as long as needed for those purposes. Web3Forms separately keeps submissions for up to three years by default. Web3Forms privacy policy. Email is handled by Google under its privacy policy.

PayPal

With your consent, PayPal forms load on plugin download pages. You can also contribute directly on PayPal. PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. is responsible for its payment service. If you contribute, PayPal sends me your name, email, amount and payment reference. I keep these to reconcile payments and answer enquiries (legitimate interests), and as required by accounting law. I retain them as long as those obligations or a specific dispute require.

The forms use PayPal Hosted Buttons for contributions and payment/security functions. PayPal’s SDK also sends loading, error and interaction measurements to PayPal. Requests disclose your IP address, browser/device details, technical identifiers and page information. Ull Hexa has no separate advertising or website analytics integration. PayPal’s wider policy also covers personalisation and advertising across its services; its published policy does not identify every internal use for this particular embed. PayPal privacy statement · PayPal cookie notice.

PayPal storage details

The reviewed SDK uses __paypal_storage__ in this site’s local storage for an identifier and session state. It has no automatic expiry in the reviewed code and remains until cleared or removed by the browser. Its internal session renews after 20 minutes; that does not delete the stored identifier. A temporary __test__localStorage__ entry checks storage availability and is immediately removed.

PayPal controls cookies on its own domains. In a fresh HTTP check of the configured form service on 20 November 2026, responses offered enforce_policy, ts and ts_c for one year; tsrce for three days; LANG for about nine hours; l7_az for 30 minutes; and session cookies x-pp-s and nsid. These are response expiry values, not proof that every browser stores every cookie. Cross-site restrictions, PayPal sign-in, region and checkout can change what is stored. This is not a complete inventory of PayPal checkout or account storage.

Browser storage

This browser remembers your cookie choice for 180 days. That period applies to Ull Hexa's choice record, not PayPal's cookies. The record contains only the choice, notice version and dates, with no name, email or visitor identifier. It is not sent to Ull Hexa.

Accept all enables the embedded PayPal forms. Reject all leaves them unloaded. Free downloads work either way. Change or withdraw your choice anytime in . Withdrawal reloads open pages where PayPal is running and stops future loading of the forms. It does not affect earlier lawful processing or delete PayPal's existing cookies or payment records. Use your browser's site-data controls and PayPal's privacy settings for those.

The preview PIN lasts for the tab session. GM Console and Studio Tools store your saved work and settings on your device until cleared or replaced. Temporary game state ends with the session. Clearing site data can remove saved games and imported assets, so export a backup first. Audio and MIDI input for visualizers is processed in your browser, not uploaded to Ull Hexa.

Hosting

GitHub serves the site and downloads and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub privacy statement. Links to music platforms and social networks open their services, where their own privacy policies apply.

Transfers

Web3Forms' operator Web3Creative is based in India. Its agreement uses EU standard contractual clauses for covered transfers. Web3Forms agreement and subprocessors.

GitHub, Google and PayPal may process information outside Norway and the EEA. Their notices describe the applicable transfer arrangements, including adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and, for PayPal group transfers, binding corporate rules.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion and, where applicable, object, restrict processing or request a portable copy. Use the contact form or email below to exercise your rights or request a copy of transfer safeguards. I may need to verify your identity. PayPal handles requests about its own payment-service records directly. You can also complain to Datatilsynet.
