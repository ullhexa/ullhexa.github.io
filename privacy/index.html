Privacy & cookies

Updated 24 November 2026

Ull Hexa handles website enquiries and the contribution records it receives. For questions or requests, use the email address on the About page or below.

Enquiries

Contact is by email to ullhexa@ik.me. Infomaniak handles enquiry email, including your email address, message and any other details you choose to send. The website no longer has a contact form. Contact is voluntary. I need your email to reply. I use these details to answer enquiries and support requests (legitimate interests), or to take steps towards a contract at your request.

I keep ordinary messages for up to 12 months after the last reply. For active projects, the 12 months start when the project and related support end. Accounting or dispute records are kept as long as needed for those purposes. Infomaniak processes enquiry email under its data processing agreement.

Earlier enquiries used Gmail and, for contact-form submissions, Web3Forms. Web3Forms’ published default retention is up to three years; removing the form does not delete those existing records. Web3Forms privacy policy.

PayPal

With your consent, PayPal forms load on plugin download pages. You can also contribute directly on PayPal. PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. is responsible for its payment service. If you contribute, PayPal sends me your name, email, amount and payment reference. I keep these to reconcile payments and answer enquiries (legitimate interests), and as required by accounting law. I retain them as long as those obligations or a specific dispute require.

The forms use PayPal Hosted Buttons for contributions and payment/security functions. PayPal’s SDK also sends loading, error and interaction measurements to PayPal. Requests disclose your IP address, browser/device details, technical identifiers and page information. PayPal’s wider policy also covers personalisation and advertising across its services; its published policy does not identify every internal use for this particular embed. PayPal privacy statement · PayPal cookie notice.

PayPal storage details

The reviewed SDK uses __paypal_storage__ in this site’s local storage for an identifier and session state. It has no automatic expiry in the reviewed code and remains until cleared or removed by the browser. Its internal session renews after 20 minutes; that does not delete the stored identifier. A temporary __test__localStorage__ entry checks storage availability and is immediately removed.

PayPal controls cookies on its own domains. In a fresh HTTP check of the configured form service on 20 November 2026, responses offered enforce_policy, ts and ts_c for one year; tsrce for three days; LANG for about nine hours; l7_az for 30 minutes; and session cookies x-pp-s and nsid. These are response expiry values, not proof that every browser stores every cookie. Cross-site restrictions, PayPal sign-in, region and checkout can change what is stored. This is not a complete inventory of PayPal checkout or account storage.

Website and tool analytics

With your separate analytics consent, I use PostHog Cloud EU to understand page visits, referral sources and campaign tags, plugin download clicks, visualizer input outcomes and visible usage time. This helps me find setup problems and improve the tools. It does not measure plugin installation or activity inside your DAW. Figures describe participating browsers, not a complete count of people.

PostHog receives a random browser identifier, session identifiers, selected event names, public product/scene identifiers, browser/device details and timing. URLs are limited to known public routes; private URL parameters are removed, apart from short campaign tags. Referrers are limited to their domain. No names, emails, contact messages, session recordings, automatic click capture, heatmaps, audio, MIDI notes, hardware names or game content are sent through analytics. Requests reach PostHog with your IP address; IP location lookup is disabled.

PostHog processes this analytics data for Ull Hexa in its EU Cloud service, hosted in Frankfurt. Browser identifiers use local storage and remain until you withdraw consent, clear them, or return after consent has expired. The current free plan has a one-year event retention period; expired events are removed in scheduled cleanup, which can take about two further months. Backups follow PostHog’s separate expiry schedule. PostHog storage and deletion · Event retention.

You can decline or withdraw analytics without affecting the site or free downloads. Withdrawal stops collection and clears our analytics identifiers from this browser; it does not automatically delete previously collected events. Analytics is not linked to your email or PayPal identity, so an email address alone cannot identify a browser’s events. Contact me if you want help with a data request.

Browser storage

This browser remembers your cookie choice for 180 days. That period applies to Ull Hexa's choice record, not PayPal's cookies or PostHog's event retention. The record contains only the choice, notice version and dates, with no name, email or visitor identifier. It is not sent to Ull Hexa.

Accept all enables PayPal forms and analytics. Reject all leaves both unloaded. Cookie settings lets you choose them separately. Free downloads work either way. Change or withdraw your choice anytime in . Withdrawal reloads open pages where PayPal is running and stops future loading of the forms. It does not affect earlier lawful processing or delete PayPal's existing cookies or payment records. Use your browser's site-data controls and PayPal's privacy settings for those.

GM Console and Studio Tools store your saved work and settings on your device until cleared or replaced. Temporary game state ends with the session. Clearing site data can remove saved games and imported assets, so export a backup first. Audio and MIDI input for visualizers is processed in your browser, not uploaded to Ull Hexa.

Hosting

GitHub serves the site and downloads and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub privacy statement. Links to music platforms and social networks open their services, where their own privacy policies apply.

Transfers

For earlier contact-form submissions, Web3Forms' operator Web3Creative is based in India. Its agreement uses EU standard contractual clauses for covered transfers. Web3Forms agreement and subprocessors.

Infomaniak is based in Switzerland. GitHub, Google (for earlier enquiries) and PayPal may process information outside Norway and the EEA. Their notices describe the applicable transfer arrangements, including adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and, for PayPal group transfers, binding corporate rules.

PostHog’s EU hosting keeps analytics event storage in the EU. Its processing agreement covers service providers and applicable transfers using standard contractual clauses where required. PostHog data processing agreement.
