Privacy & cookies

Updated 20 November 2026

Ull Hexa is the independent artist and developer in Norway responsible for this website and for the messages and contribution records described here. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form or write to ullhexa.music@gmail.com.

There are no visitor accounts or first-party analytics or advertising trackers. The optional PayPal panel is covered separately below. You can browse and download the free products without submitting your name or email. Hosting providers still receive technical information when serving pages and files.

Contact messages

The form sends your name, email address, subject and message through Web3Forms to my Gmail inbox. Web3Forms also receives technical information such as your IP address and submission time, and uses spam filtering. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.

I use this information to respond to you and manage the conversation. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in handling inquiries and support, or taking steps at your request before a contract where that applies. Providing contact details is voluntary, but I need your email to reply. Messages are not used for mailing lists or marketing.

Ordinary emails in my mailbox are deleted within 12 months after the last reply. Active project conversations are retained while the project and related support remain active, then follow the same 12-month rule. Records needed to meet a legal obligation or resolve a specific dispute may be retained for that purpose and period.

Web3Forms has a separate provider copy: its published default is up to three years. Its processing is governed by its privacy policy and data processing agreement. Web3Creative operates from India and uses infrastructure and spam-filtering providers, including CleanTalk or Akismet. Its agreement provides EU standard contractual clauses for relevant international transfers. Email is handled by Google under its privacy policy.

Optional PayPal contributions

PayPal is not loaded automatically on plugin download pages. The payment panel only loads after you select Allow and load PayPal. You can select Keep PayPal off, use the separate PayPal website link, or download the plugin without contributing.

Loading the panel connects your browser to PayPal. PayPal receives your IP address and browser/device details and may use cookies and similar storage for payments, fraud prevention, personalisation, measurement and advertising, depending on your PayPal settings. PayPal may associate this activity with your PayPal account. Read its privacy statement and cookie notice before enabling it.

Your permission applies to the current page visit only. Use Turn PayPal off and reload below the panel to stop it on this page. Reloading or closing the page also resets this choice. This does not delete cookies PayPal has already placed; use your browser's site-data controls and PayPal's privacy settings for those. Withdrawing permission does not undo processing that already happened.

If you contribute, PayPal processes the payment and gives me transaction information, which may include your name, email, amount and transaction identifier. I use it to reconcile contributions, handle payment questions and meet applicable record-keeping duties, based on legitimate interests and legal obligations. I do not receive your full card details. PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. controls its payment-service records and retention, separately from Ull Hexa.

Hosting, downloads and external links

GitHub Pages hosts this site and GitHub Releases serves plugin installers. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security and processes request/device information under its privacy statement. I do not use these requests to build visitor profiles. Links to music platforms and social networks open their services, where their own privacy policies apply.

GitHub, Google and PayPal may process information outside Norway and the EEA. Their notices describe the applicable transfer arrangements, including adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and, for PayPal group transfers, binding corporate rules. Contact me if you need more information about a provider used for your inquiry.

Storage on your device

  • Preview PIN: a session-storage value remembers that you unlocked the preview in that tab. It ends with the browser session.
  • GM Console: game state, local maps and other assets, and interface preferences are kept in local storage or IndexedDB on your device so the game can run and resume. Some temporary state uses session storage. Persistent data stays until you reset it or clear site data. Clearing it can remove saved games and imported assets, so export a backup first.
  • Studio Tools: a saved studio layout stays in local storage until replaced or cleared.

This storage supports the features you choose to use. It is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Audio and MIDI input for visualizers is processed in your browser, not uploaded to Ull Hexa. Browser permissions control microphone, tab-audio and MIDI access.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent where it is the basis for processing. Contact me using the details above; I may need to verify that the request concerns your information. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.
